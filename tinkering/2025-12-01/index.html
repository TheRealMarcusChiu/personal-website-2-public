How to Enable IOMMU on ASROCK ROMED8-2T

  1. December 1, 2025
  2. 1 minutes

You will have to enable it in both:

  • BIOS
  • GRUB

Enable in BIOS

IOMMU is under Advanced > AMD CBS > NBIO Common Options.

Enable in GRUB

vim /etc/default/grub

You will see the line with GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX_DEFAULT="quiet", all you need to do is add intel_iommu=on or amd_iommu=on depending on your system.

# Should look like this
GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX_DEFAULT="quiet intel_iommu=on"

Update grub and reboot system

update-grub
shutdown -r now

Now check to make sure everything is enabled.

dmesg | grep -e DMAR -e IOMMU
dmesg | grep 'remapping'

Should look something like the following
