Better Unsaid

  1. July 4, 2025
  2. 3 minutes

Looking back from the beginning till the end of us, I’ve learned some surprising things about myself that I didn’t even realize were possible for me.

I didn’t realize how focused I can be in trying to understand her character, her likes and dislikes:

  • she is soft-spoken at first, prefers 1 on 1 hangouts over groups, but once well-acquainted she enjoys “spilling the tea” so to speak, bantering, and making unintelligible playful noises which she labels as “demon sounds” (not sure why she stopped doing that overtime, it’s the weirdness of her character that I’ve liked)
  • she would at times be anxious in group settings, and other times be self-conscious of her hair when it’s windy, susceptible to love-bombing especially in vulnerable times, and her love language is first touch then quality time.
  • she likes lego botanical sets, thin minimalistic metal bracelets, purses, perfumes, TikTok, and yarn-knitted clothes especially in the winter (as she wore them back-to-back during the second and third times we’ve met; a soft white one at a coffee shop. and a striped blue-gray one when we got pho)

Overtime, I would get her several gifts and even her birthday gift 6 months in advance lol. I thought about gifts that aligned to her likes and especially the ones that keeps on giving. So I’ve made her a bracelet with interchangeable lego plates. So as we shared more experiences; those experiences can be eternalized into plates. I’ve intentionally left one empty for her upcoming birthday.

However, I’ve remembered she said something about “love bombing”, so I’ve held back not wanting to do that to her. Perhaps this was a sign I was holding on to a bomb for myself just waiting to explode. I’ve ended up keeping over a half dozen gifts to myself, holding back in other areas - keeping at an arms distance; not wanting to mess with her love language. Although, there were times I’ve let some slip by.

I didn’t realize how much emotions could take reign over me. Especially after I called things off - the next day or two emotions flooded to the point where I would make up a lie and blame-shift a fictitious person. My clouded judgement thought it would help but it turned out to be the proverbial final nail for her, perhaps the entire coffin itself. It wasn’t mentioned - maybe it was necessary - in helping us move on.

Perhaps one day, someone will meet my patience and close that chapter. So a new one can open up, where I can express the un-expressed, in a faithful manner.
